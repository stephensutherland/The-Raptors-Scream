## 🛠️ Field Assessment Checklist

### 1. Natural Surveillance & Spatial Visibility
*The objective is to eliminate areas where an attacker can hide or compress a victim out of public view.*

*   [ ] **Sidewalk Clearances:** Verify if weeds, wild bushes, or hedges exceed **3 feet in height** within 15 feet of walking pathways.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Organize a localized clearance team to chop brush down to a uniform 3-foot ceiling to preserve sightlines from neighboring porches.
*   [ ] **Tree Canopy Obstructions:** Inspect if low-hanging branches block municipal streetlights or obscure second-story observation windows.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Limb tree trunks up to **7 feet from ground level**.
*   [ ] **Blind Alley Vectors:** Identify non-reflective corners, recessed building entries, or unmonitored footpaths between houses.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Mount weather-proof 12-inch outdoor convex security mirrors on utility poles or building edges to expose hidden angles.

### 2. Physical Property Reclamation
*Securing vacant land and abandoned structures prevents attackers from establishing staging environments.*

*   [ ] **Vacant Structure Breach Inspection:** Document every abandoned or unmanaged home within a 4-block radius. Check for broken ground-level windows or unsecured back doors.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Secure entry points using 3/4-inch plywood sheets fastened with heavy-duty hex-head structural screws. Fasten external padlocks to main exterior doors.
*   [ ] **Perimeter Vehicle Breaches:** Look for empty fields or side yards where a non-resident vehicle can idle dark or pull completely out of sight from main transit lines.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Place large rock barriers, heavy wooden planters, or steel pickets to physically prevent unauthorized vehicle entry.
*   [ ] **Sub-Structure Isolation Access:** Ensure crawlspaces, storage sheds, and spaces under porches are fully enclosed.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Install wire mesh or durable lattice paneling around exposed porch underbellies to prevent physical concealment.

### 3. Illumination Audit (Execute Strictly Post-Twilight)
*Predators utilize darkness to bypass community awareness. Lighting forces accountability.*

*   [ ] **Public Infrastructure Outages:** Map dead streetlights or flickering municipal fixtures on standard walking paths.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Record the serial number printed on the metal streetlight pole and report it to the city utility department daily.
*   [ ] **High-Risk Dark Corridors:** Identify areas where unhoused residents or late-shift workers are forced to transit through total shadow.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Install independent, high-lumen solar-powered motion-activated floodlights onto cooperative neighbors' fences, trees, or outbuildings.
*   [ ] **Abandoned Backyard Shadow Isolation:** Check if the rears of empty properties are completely pitch black.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Rig commercial-grade, solar-charging flood lamps to high tree branches facing downward directly onto the vacant property's backyard.

### 4. Target Hardening & Decentralized Evidence Capture
*Creating an off-grid tracking ring ensures documentation that local entities cannot manipulate or delete.*

*   [ ] **Choke-Point Surveillance:** Review the main ingress and egress arterial roads leading into the sector.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Deploy cellular-independent, high-definition trail cameras hidden inside camouflage boxes on private properties facing oncoming traffic lanes to document all vehicle license plates.
*   [ ] **Vulnerable Hotspot Monitoring:** Ensure isolated parking areas, dead ends, or communal water collection points are covered.
    *   *Remediation Task:* Mount camouflaged micro-SD tracking cameras high up in tree lines, safely out of arms reach, using locking security cables.
